Udemy and the Probabilistic Robotics site block scripts, so I confirmed those
two through search results.

Budget: 10–15 hours a week for four weeks, so about 40–60 hours in total. The
times below add up to about 45 hours if you do only the "start here" items and
the parts marked "do". Everything is free unless it says paid.

Order to follow, matching the learning path in [README.md](README.md):

- Week 9: topic 1 (ROS 2 core), 2 (TF2), 5 (MuJoCo), 7 (tooling)
- Week 10: topic 6 (sim-to-real)
- Week 11: topic 3 (Nav2), 4 (theory: planning, costmaps)
- Week 12: topic 3 again (costmap layers), 4 (MPC)

## 4. Mobile robotics theory (planning, control, costmaps)

Start with: **Cyrill Stachniss's "Mobile Sensing and Robotics 1" lectures,
picked videos only**.

Localization and SLAM are out of scope, so skip the Bayes filter, Kalman filter,
particle filter, EKF localization and SLAM videos even though they are the most
famous part of his channel.

| Rank | Resource                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Format         | Time             | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | [Mobile Sensing and Robotics 1 (Winter 2021/22, Uni Bonn)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgnQpQtFTOGQEn33QDVGJpiZLi-SlL7vA), Cyrill Stachniss and lab. Free.                                                                                       | Video lectures | ~6 h             | Watch: Occupancy Grid Maps (1 h 9 min; what a costmap grid is), Robot Motion Planning using A\* (1 h 38 min; what Smac does), Motion Models (41 min), Robot Locomotion (56 min), Robot Control (1 h 25 min), Model Predictive Control Part 1 (Lasse Peters, 42 min; the idea under MPPI). Optional: the 5-minute "Occupancy Grid" short. Also useful: [Self-Driving Cars playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgnQpQtFTOGQo2Z_ogbonywTg8jxCI9pD), "Planning" (54 min). There is a newer [Robot Control Lecture (2025)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgnQpQtFTOGT_BlLxVnsuI-7CEQCgNuk4) on the same channel; Control-03 (wheeled robot models, 1 h 8 min) and Control-06 (MPC, 45 min) are good substitutes for the older control videos. |
| 2    | [Modern Robotics](https://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics), Lynch & Park. Free PDF and videos. [Coursera specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/modernrobotics) is paid for graded work; auditing gives the videos. | Book + video   | 4–6 h            | Ch. 10 Motion Planning: 10.1–10.2 (configuration space, obstacles), 10.4 (grid methods, A\*). Ch. 13.3–13.4 (motion planning and feedback control for wheeled robots). This is Coursera Course 4 and Course 5. Skip manipulator chapters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3    | [Planning Algorithms](https://lavalle.pl/planning/), Steven M. LaValle (2006). Free online book.                                                                                                                                                            | Book           | 2–3 h, reference | Use as a reference, not a read-through. Ch. 2 (discrete planning, A\*), Ch. 14.1–14.3 (planning with differential constraints, why Hybrid-A\*/lattice planners exist).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

Not picked:

- *Probabilistic Robotics* (Thrun, Burgard, Fox, MIT Press, 2005) is the
  classic, but it is a paid book and mostly about localization and SLAM. If you
  have library access, Ch. 5 (motion models) and Ch. 9 (occupancy grid mapping)
  are the relevant parts. The
  [book site](http://www.probabilistic-robotics.org/) blocks scripts.
- [Underactuated Robotics](https://underactuated.mit.edu/) (Russ Tedrake, MIT)
  is the gold standard for trajectory optimization and MPC, but it is too deep
  for four weeks. The
  [trajectory optimization chapter](https://underactuated.mit.edu/trajopt.html)
  is the place to go after the project.

## 5. MuJoCo

Start with: **the official tutorial Colab notebook**.

MuJoCo 4.0.1 (Nov 11, 2026) is the current release per the
[changelog](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/changelog.html). There is no
official DeepMind video course. The notebooks and docs are the standard.

| Rank | Resource                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Format         | Time  | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | [MuJoCo Python tutorial notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/github/google-deepmind/mujoco/blob/main/python/tutorial.ipynb), Google DeepMind. Free.                                                                       | Colab notebook | 3–4 h | Do: MuJoCo basics (mjModel, named access, mjData), Basic rendering/simulation/animation, Simulation (degrees of freedom), Timestep and accuracy, Contacts and Friction, Tendons/actuators/sensors, Advanced rendering (camera matrix, camera control; you need this for the camera node and for calibration). Skim the tippe-top and chaotic pendulum examples. |
| 2    | [MuJoCo docs: Overview](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/overview.html), [Modeling](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/modeling.html), [Python bindings](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/python.html). Free. | Reference      | 3 h   | Overview: read in full. Modeling: kinematic tree, joints, actuators, cameras, lights, materials and textures (what you randomize). Python: the viewer, rendering, and the 4.0 package layout note. Keep the [XML reference](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/XMLreference.html) open while writing the robot file.                                       |
| 3    | [mjSpec model editing notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/github/google-deepmind/mujoco/blob/main/python/mjspec.ipynb), Google DeepMind. Free.                                                                           | Colab notebook | 1–2 h | Building and changing models from Python. This is how to generate rooms and randomize scenes in the data engine without string-templating XML. Do it in week 10.                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Also handy:
[MuJoCo Menagerie](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie) for
ready-made robot and camera models to copy patterns from.
[MuJoCo MPC](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_mpc) shows predictive
sampling, close in spirit to MPPI, but it is optional.

Free.                     | Paper         | 1 h        | Closest to your project: object detection trained on randomized synthetic images, then tested on real photos. [Domain Randomization for Sim2Real Transfer](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2019-05-05-domain-randomization/)

blog post (openai.com/index/learning-dexterity) blocked my fetch, so I could not
confirm it is still up.

## Outdated or moved

- **Nav2 docs URLs changed.** docs.nav2.org now uses `/jazzy/`, `/rolling/` and
  similar paths. Old deep links (`/tuning/index.html`,
  `/getting_started/index.html`, `/configuration/packages/...`) return 404.
- **Articulated Robotics** is Foxy-era with Gazebo Classic. The concepts hold,
  but the commands and simulator steps are out of date for Jazzy.
- **pixi's ROS 2 tutorial** still uses the Humble channel.
- **Robotics Back-End free YouTube/blog ROS 2 material** is mostly Foxy/Humble.
  Only the paid Udemy course is updated for Jazzy.
- **Probabilistic Robotics** is still the classic, but it is paid and mostly
  about SLAM and localization.
- **OpenAI "Learning Dexterity" post** could not be fetched (403), so I could
  not confirm it is still up.
- **No official MuJoCo video course** exists. Use the notebooks.
